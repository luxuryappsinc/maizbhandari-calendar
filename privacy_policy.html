Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Luxury Applications ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Maizbhandari Calendar mobile application ("App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This App is designed primarily to provide calendar, event, reminder, and related information features.

Important: Maizbhandari Calendar does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, or other personal information to use the main features of the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, certain information may be stored or processed.

1.1 Events and Calendar Information

The App allows you to create your own events. Information you enter into an event may include:

  • Event title
  • Event description
  • Date and time
  • Event type
  • Location
  • Repeat settings
  • Reminder settings
  • Images that you choose to attach to an event

User-created event information is intended to remain on your device and is used to provide the calendar and reminder functionality of the App.

1.2 App Settings and Preferences

The App may store settings such as language preferences, favorites, and other application preferences locally on your device.

1.3 Remote Calendar Data

The App may retrieve publicly provided calendar and event information from our remote servers. This information is used to keep built-in Maizbhandari events and related calendar information up to date.

Remote calendar data does not require you to submit personal information.

2. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App may schedule notifications on your device for events and reminders that you configure.

These notifications are generated by the App and operating system according to your selected event and reminder settings.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

3. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information about ad interactions, in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

We do not control the information collected directly by advertising providers. Their collection and use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.
